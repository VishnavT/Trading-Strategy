Developed a Python-based investment backtesting project using yfinance and pandas to retrieve and analyze historical stock market data. Implemented moving-average crossover strategies to generate hypothetical buy and sell signals, initially testing the strategy on Apple before expanding the analysis to 20 years of S&P 500 ETF (SPY) data. Created visualizations of historical prices and portfolio equity curves to examine how investment strategies performed over time. This project introduced me to quantitative investing, technical analysis, and the importance of evaluating investment strategies using historical data
